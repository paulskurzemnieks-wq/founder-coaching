The Method

The FRAME Method

Five pillars that turn generic value-posting into content only you could have made. And only your ideal customer would care about.

F
Focus
Know your ideal customer deeper than age and industry. Their day, their decisions, their fears, their ambitions. The stuff most brands never bother with.
R
Repetition
Reinforce the same positioning until the market can't picture the category without you in it.
A
Authority
Your external and internal Outliers. Background, results, beliefs, how you actually think. The things no one can copy off you.
M
Mechanism
Don't tell them what to do. Show them why it works. Belief shift beats another value post every time.
E
Emotion
Information doesn't move people. Feeling does. Every post should make the right person feel, rethink, or reframe.

You can. Agencies are useful. But an agency has many clients. You're one of many accounts. I work with you one on one, learn your business, your customer, your positioning, and give feedback tied to your specific situation. Not another generic post designed for a mass audience.

Plan for one 60-minute call per week plus 2 to 4 hours of implementation (recording, editing decisions, message reviews). Async WhatsApp support handles the smaller stuff so we don't burn call time on it.

For personal-brand founders, yes. Content that positions you eventually has you in it. If you're camera-shy, we'll build up to it and shape a format that fits you. I've walked business owners from "I hate this" to running their own shoots in weeks.
